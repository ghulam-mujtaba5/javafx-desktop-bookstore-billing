## 1. Overview
Bookstore & Stationery Billing POS is an offline-first desktop application engineered for local retail bookstore and stationery management. Your privacy and data sovereignty are paramount.

## 2. Data Collection and Storage
* **Local Data Storage:** All business data—including inventory catalogs, products, customer profiles, sales invoices, stock adjustments, and financial Z-reports—is stored locally on the user's computer in an embedded SQLite database (`bookstore_master.db`).
* **Zero Telemetry / No Tracking:** The application does not collect, track, transmit, or monetize any personal data, business analytics, transaction histories, or payment details.
* **Bibliographic Lookups:** When scanning or adding books by ISBN, the application may optionally query public bibliographic APIs (Google Books API and Open Library) over HTTPS to fetch public book metadata (title, author, publisher, cover art). No user identity or store information is sent with these queries.

## 3. Third-Party Services & Payment Processing
* The application operates locally and does not process external credit cards directly through cloud servers. Cash, card, and store credit transactions are logged locally on the merchant's machine for bookkeeping and invoice generation.
* No personal customer information is shared with third parties or external marketing networks.

## 4. Data Security & Backups
* All records remain entirely within the merchant's local machine or designated local backup directory.
* The merchant retains 100% control, ownership, and responsibility over their database files.
